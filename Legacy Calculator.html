ENGINE: WAITING FOR INPUT

1. What This Tool Does

This institutional-grade engine stress-tests your retirement plan to answer one specific question: How much financial Legacy will you leave behind at the end of your life?
Instead of assuming a steady, safe return, it subjects your assets and spending goals to 1,000 real-world historical market sequences. It calculates exactly how much wealth you will leave behind under average median conditions, and precisely how much you can safely spend without running out of money during a severe, multi-year market crash (like the Great Depression or the Dot-Com bust). All outputs are shown in Today's Pounds (real purchasing power).

2. The Glide Phase

The Glide is a period of intentional financial freedom between stepping away from a high-stress career and officially reaching Retirement Age. During this phase, you bridge the gap between a lower "post-exit income" and your actual living expenses. The engine dynamically drains your assets in a strict, tax-efficient sequence to fund this gap: Cash Buffer → Taxable Stocks → ISAs → Pension.

3. Post-Retirement: The Phased Drawdown

After you reach Retirement Age and pay off your remaining mortgage, the engine steps your spending down through three distinct phases of aging:

  • The Go-Go Years: Maximum health and physical activity, heavy discretionary travel. Highest spend.
  • The Slow-Go Years: Transitioning closer to home, fewer major international trips. Reduced spend.
  • The No-Go Years: Baseline living costs and local activity until your defined Longevity Horizon.

4. Institutional Mechanics: Tax, Guardrails & Correlated Assets

To provide professional-grade accuracy, this engine does not rely on heuristic fudges. It utilizes three advanced systems:

  • Dual-Asset Stochastic Correlation: Taxable accounts and Growth ISAs track an aggressive 100% Equity array. The Bond ISA tracks a parallel 100% Sovereign Bond array. The Pension tracks a 60/40 blended model. This perfectly captures real-world diversification and flight-to-safety mechanics during market shocks.
  • HMRC Marginal Solver: Pension drawdowns are routed through a marginal tax algorithm solving for your exact UK income band (accounting for 25% Tax-Free Cash, Basic, and Higher rate thresholds) based on your baseline income and State Pension status in any given year.
  • Adaptive Guyton-Klinger Guardrails: If a market crash drives your Current Withdrawal Rate 20% higher than your Initial Withdrawal Rate, the engine automatically throttles your discretionary Go-Go spending by 10% to protect the asset base, simulating dynamic human discipline rather than rigid depletion. Furthermore, during negative equity years, the engine actively bypasses your stocks and drains your Bond ISA to protect your shares for the recovery.

5. The Equity Release Solver

If your requested lifestyle triggers a mathematical failure in a 10th percentile crash (a Great Depression scenario), the engine acts as a Capital Solver. It calculates the exact minimum Equity Release required at your specified Downsize Age to inject cash, protect your portfolio, and save the plan.
